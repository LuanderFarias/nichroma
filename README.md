~ nichroma [in development]

minimal game library manager & launcher with multiple sources, plugins and community projects.

<img src="/.github/desktop.png"></img>
<img src="/.github/bigpicture.png"></img>
> nichroma is a spiritual successor of [cheep](https://github.com/LuanderFarias/Cheep)

nichroma is a simple-to-use, minimal game library manager & launcher that integrates with official clients and community tools, eliminating the need to spend hours configuring launchers, organizing emulators, manually removing uninstalled games, and more.

made to be local-first, customizable and fully extendable by the community. you can suggest features, plugins and sources by opening an [issue](https://github.com/LuanderFarias/nichroma/issues/new).

we're also planning integrations with fex, proton, dxvk/vkd3D, and others for a possible android version, if you have experience with these types of integrations please get in touch through our [discord](https://discord.gg/VRBVsjJ7NQ).

~ plugins

plugins are a huge part of nichroma, they're made by the community and/or our team to extend nichroma functionalities and make your overall experience better.
| plugin | description | authors |
| :-------- | :-------- | :-------- |
